# Battlegrounds Upgrade

Open `Assets/Rebuild/Scenes/BattlegroundsUpgrade.unity` and press Play. This is an independent tavern preparation scene connected to `SiegeTavernBattle.unity`, not a replacement for the formal campaign's UpgradeScene or the earlier SiegeTavernUpgrade prototype.

## Controls and rules

- Buy an offer for 3 gold. It enters the bench. Drag the offer directly onto a formation slot to buy and deploy in one action.
- Click a bench unit, then a formation slot; or drag the unit onto a slot. Occupied slots swap. The 3x3 formation supports six deployed units, with seven bench spaces.
- Drag a deployed unit to the bench, or select it and click TO BENCH. Select and SELL, or drag onto SELL, for 1 gold. The unique starting Captain cannot be sold, and the last deployed unit cannot be benched or sold.
- Refresh costs 1 gold. Freeze preserves unsold offers for the next preparation. Refresh unfreezes the shop.
- Tavern tiers 1–6 unlock the project's existing units. A larger shop appears on the next refresh: three offers at tiers 1–2, four at 3–4 and five at 5–6. Existing offers are not discarded when upgrading.
- Upgrade cost decreases as turns pass. Each turn supplies fresh gold (3 initially, increasing to a maximum of 10); unspent gold does not carry over. There is no interest.
- Three identical ordinary units automatically become one golden unit. Golden units have double attack, maximum health and healing output, retain an existing deployed copy's slot if possible, and award a free discovery.
- Discovery offers up to three distinct existing roles, preferring the next tavern tier. The small role catalog fills the remaining choices from lower tiers. Choose one for free; the preparation timer pauses during discovery.
- Unique legendary support units cannot be duplicated or tripled. Golden copies do not combine further. This prototype samples offers independently; it does not implement a shared finite pool.
- Preparation lasts 75 seconds. READY begins the assault early. Existing units move and fight automatically against the enemy construction system. Legendary support skill buttons appear in combat; Time Lock targets the nearest living tower area.
- Battle returns to this preparation scene and preserves recruits, golden status and formation. Units recover between assaults. Eight assaults or three losses end the expedition. Progress is held in memory only.

## Existing resources

Names, role icons, descriptions and base combat stats come from `LegacyUnitLooks`, `LegacyUnitIconUI`, `LegacyUnitIntroductionUI` and `SquadCombatRules`. Combat uses the existing Catgirl role visuals already configured in SiegeTavernBattle. The starting units are Captain and Attack; no new follower class or external art was introduced.

## Implementation

- `Scripts/Presentation/Minimal/TavernRecruitment.cs`: isolated economy, formation, triple and discovery state.
- `Scripts/Presentation/Minimal/BattlegroundsUpgradeScene.cs`: uGUI/TMP scene UI and drag/drop.
- `Scripts/Presentation/Minimal/SiegeTavernMode.cs`: opt-in bridge to the existing standalone battle and return scene.
- `Editor/BattlegroundsUpgradeBuilder.cs`: creates the new scene without overwriting existing scenes.
- `Tests/EditMode/TavernRecruitmentTests.cs`, `Tests/PlayMode/BattlegroundsUpgradeTests.cs`: behavior and scene integration checks.
- `Logs/Battlegrounds`: local test results and rendered UI previews.

For an isolated player build, use a separate Build Profile containing BattlegroundsUpgrade and SiegeTavernBattle, with BattlegroundsUpgrade first. The existing campaign build list was not changed. Web deployment and long-run balance have not been validated.

## Simplified board layout

The UI uses a single central tavern board: shop offers across the top, the 3x3 formation in the center, the bench below, and the current player's Captain, lives and gold along the bottom. Upgrade, refresh and freeze sit above the shop; the timer sits at the right. There is no opponent roster, leaderboard or seven-player sidebar. Click a shop card to inspect its existing unit ability before buying.


The board silhouette, concave frame, blue-gray lower wings, arched portraits, shield buttons and stat badges are now flat procedural UI polygons. No reference-game artwork or textures are included. Geometry is generated by TavernPolygonGraphic; gameplay and the single-player-only layout are unchanged.
